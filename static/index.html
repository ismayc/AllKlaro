🗣️ AllKlaro

Live, fully-local speech translation between German, English, and Spanish. Speech recognition, voice detection, and translation all run on this machine — nothing is sent to the cloud.

Tap a translation's 📋 to copy it, or its ✏️ to fix it; corrections are remembered and steer future translations. Long-press any word for its dictionary entry — gender, plural, and meanings.
