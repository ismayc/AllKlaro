🗣️ AllKlaro

Live, fully-local speech translation between German, English, and Spanish. Speech recognition, voice detection, and translation all run on this machine — nothing is sent to the cloud.

Tap a translation's 📋 to copy it, or its ✏️ to fix it; corrections are remembered and steer future translations. Long-press any word for its dictionary entry — gender, plural, and meanings.

Tap ✨ to re-translate a card with the main model. During a conversation the second pass has to beat the next card to the finish and is skipped when the pipeline is behind; asking for it by hand gives it no deadline at all, which is when the bigger model is worth the wait. Marked improved when it lands, even if the answer came back the same.

Tap ⏪ for what did they just say? — the last 45 seconds are rejoined and translated as one passage. German puts the verb at the end of a clause, so a thought cut into two cards can leave each half untranslatable on its own: “…they put gravel in the garden” and “spread out so the water drains better” become “they spread gravel in the garden area so the water drains better”. It reads the cards already on screen, so it costs nothing and changes nothing.

A dashed new voice line appears between cards when the voice changes. It says only that — with one microphone for a whole room, who is speaking is not something this can know, and it does not guess.

In an auto mode each card's language chip is a button: tap it to redo that card the other way round. A dashed chip means the guess was close. The Auto button by the type bar pins the language you are typing so it is never guessed at all.
